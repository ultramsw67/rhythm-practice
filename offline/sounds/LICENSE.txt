Instrument sounds (sounds/*.js)
-------------------------------
Source: "FluidR3_GM" SoundFont by Frank Wen, as pre-rendered by the
"MIDI.js Soundfonts" project (FluidR3_GM, MP3 in JavaScript),
https://github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts

License of the pre-rendered sounds: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/us/)
License of the original SoundFont: MIT (full text below)

Changes made for this app: only the notes inside each wind instrument's
playing range were kept, one note every 3 semitones (notes in between are
produced by changing the playback speed). The audio itself was not edited.
Euphonium uses the tuba sound; C-instrument parts use the acoustic grand piano.
